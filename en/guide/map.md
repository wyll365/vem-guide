# Device Map

Menu: **Devices › Device Map**

![Device Map](/images/map.png)

The map gives you an at-a-glance view of where all your devices are and how they are doing:

- Header stats: total devices, online (green), offline (red), and no-location devices.
- Map markers: green = online, red = offline; **only devices that already have coordinates appear on the map**.
- Click a marker to see the device name, code, and status, then click **Detail** to open the device detail page.

::: tip Where do device coordinates come from?
GPS reported by the device is used first; without GPS, the location is estimated from the IP address (which can be quite inaccurate). To correct a location manually, go to **Device Detail › Device Info**, drag the map marker, and save — the map page itself is view-only.
:::
